Naat Companion: Privacy Policy

Oriva Apps · Last updated 15 November 2026

Naat Companion is an app for reading naat, salaam and manqabat in Urdu, Hindi and Roman. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your favourites, recently read kalaam, setlists, guldastas, recitals, reading positions and settings (such as the app language and text size) are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Internet

All kalaam are included in the app, so it works without an internet connection. The app does not send any data over the internet.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: orivaapps@gmail.com
