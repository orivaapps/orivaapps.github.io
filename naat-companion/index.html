Naat Companion

Hamd, naat, salaam and manqabat of our akabir in Urdu, Hindi and Roman, with stage mode for the mehfil.

Get it on Google Play

Need help, or found a mistake in a kalaam?
Write to orivaapps@gmail.com. For a mistake, tell us the kalaam's name and the misra, and we'll correct it in the next update.

How do I find a kalaam when I only remember one line?

Type any part of any misra in the search, in Roman, Hindi or Urdu. The spelling doesn't have to be exact.

How do I read in a different script?

Open a kalaam and use its script switch to choose Urdu, Hindi, Roman, or Urdu with Roman under each line. The app's own language (English, Hindi or Urdu) is chosen on the home screen.

What is stage mode?

Large text that scrolls by itself while you recite. The screen stays on, and you can change the speed or pause at any time.

Do I need internet?

No. Every kalaam is inside the app and works offline.

Where are my favourites and setlists saved?

Only on your phone. There is no account, so they are not backed up. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Where does the text come from?

The Urdu text is from the books published by Maktaba-tul-Madina (Dawat-e-Islami).
